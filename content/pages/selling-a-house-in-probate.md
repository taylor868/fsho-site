<section class="wrap pad"><h2>Probate is the court process</h2><p>You may be the executor, an heir, or the family member who ended up with the keys. The house may still be full. Nobody has to have every answer before calling.</p><p>Probate is how a court handles a person's estate. A house in that estate may need a signature from someone the court recognizes. It may need the court's approval before it can be sold. Those rules change by state and by estate.</p><p>We can talk about the property. We cannot tell you whether you may sign, whether the court has to approve a sale, or what the estate should do. We are not attorneys. Ask the estate's attorney or the court before you rely on a sale.</p></section>

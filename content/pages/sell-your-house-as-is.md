<section class="wrap pad"><h2>As-is means the house as it sits today</h2><p>Selling a house as is means you can ask for an offer without fixing, painting, or cleaning first. A leak, a dated kitchen, or a room full of boxes does not have to become your project before you call.</p><p>You can leave what you do not want. We will tell you plainly if the house is not a fit. There is no cost to ask, and no obligation to accept an offer.</p></section>

Tell us the condition in plain words.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>No cleaning day</h3><p>You do not have to empty the house or make it show-ready. An as-is conversation is not an open house.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>No guess at a price</h3><p>We do not put an offer amount on this page. Every house is different. We show you both.</p><p>We buy single-family houses and multi-family properties with 2–4 units.

<section class="wrap pad"><h2>The house, not the dispute</h2><p>Divorce is about two people moving on. The house is often stuck in the middle. One person may still live there. Repairs may have stopped. Neither person may want to host showings.</p><p>We can talk about a sale. We cannot tell you who should keep the house, who signs, or how a court will divide anything. We are not attorneys. Those questions stay with your lawyers and the court.</p></section>

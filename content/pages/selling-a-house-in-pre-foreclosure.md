<section class="wrap pad"><h2>This is the earlier stage</h2><p>Pre-foreclosure, on this page, means you are behind and you still have time to look around. You may be getting letters. You may be avoiding the phone. That is a hard spot, and it is more common than it feels. There is no shame in it.</p><p>This is not our foreclosure page. If a sale date is already on the calendar, start at <a href="/stop-foreclosure/">stop foreclosure</a>. Stay here if you are earlier than that and you want to see options while there is still room on the calendar.</p></section>

<section class="alt"><div class="wrap pad"><h2>While there is still time, start with the loan</h2><p>At this earlier stage the useful first calls are not a race to sell. Ask your lender what they can do about the missed payments: a repayment plan, forbearance, or a loan modification. We are not your lender. We cannot change the loan, and we cannot make them pause anything.</p><p>Then talk with someone who does this for free and is not trying to buy the house. HUD-approved housing counselors are that resource. Start at <a href="https://www.hud.gov/counseling">hud.gov/counseling</a>. If you have a legal question, talk with a foreclosure attorney. We are not attorneys or housing counselors.</p><p class="fine">Selling does not guarantee that a foreclosure stops, and it does not guarantee what happens to your credit.</p></div></section>

<section class="wrap pad"><h2>Where an offer fits, if you still want one</h2><p>Some people at this stage want a sale so they can move the debt off their plate before a sale date exists. That can be a fit when the numbers work. It is not the only way through, and it is not a rescue plan.</p><p>If you ask us, we look at the house as it sits. No repairs and no cleaning are required just to ask. There is no cost, and no obligation to accept. If you do accept, a title company sends the payoff at closing. If the dates or the loan make a sale a poor fit, we will say so.</p><p>If a sale date is already set, do not use this page as your guide. Use <a href="/stop-foreclosure/">stop foreclosure</a>.
